Continuous Learning of the Dynamics of a Double Pendulum by World Model Hamiltonian Constraint
Axel Bröns

Implementation a continuous generative world model that predicts the chaotic dynamics of a double pendulum directly from raw videos. By integrating a Hamiltonian Neural Network (HNN) into the heart of a Neural ODE, the latent space learns to strictly respect energy conservation laws without any direct physical supervision.

Graph-Aware Transformers for Smart Contract Vulnerability Detection

Detect automaticaly anomalies and fraudulent codes in smart contracts using a graph approach coupled with transformers and a classification layer. Our model achieves 0.81 F1 Score and 0.91 Recall.

Detection of Academic Cognitive Stress Using Machine Learning
Axel Bröns, Anas Boutaleb, Omar El Alami El Fellousse

This research project proposes an objective methodology for measuring and anticipating stress in the school environment. By relying on the acquisition of biomedical signals and supervised learning algorithms, we identify 88.15% (F1 score) of mental states overload to prevent the risks of burn-out and academic dropout.
